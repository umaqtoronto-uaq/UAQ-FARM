We're off to the farm!

A fall day of pumpkins, barn animals and hands-on learning at Forsythe Family Farms.

Assalamu alaikum, dear families,

We are so excited to take our students to Forsythe Family Farms next week. The farm's friendly educators will guide the children through the pumpkin patch, the barn and the garden, so they can see where their food comes from and have a wonderful time doing it.

Each child will pick a pie pumpkin to bring home. Below are a few easy ideas to help the day go smoothly. Thank you for helping us get ready!

For students not joining the trip

Thursday, October 8 is a holiday for them. Please keep them at home that day.

Fall fun, rain or shineThe farm works around the weather to keep the children comfortable. The forecast shows a cooler day with a chance of light rain on Thursday, so a jacket or raincoat over the uniform means fun in any weather. Please check the forecast on Wednesday night.

Dress for the fun

  • School uniform for everyone on the trip.
  • A jacket and sweater to suit the weather. Layers are perfect, since they can come off if the sun comes out.
  • Running shoes, comfy for walking around the farm.
  • The farm is usually a bit cooler and windier than the city, so a warm layer is a good idea.

Pack for the day

  • A popsicle stick or name card with your child's name, so their plant is easy to find.
  • A reusable bag for the pumpkin they will pick.
  • A lunch, a snack and a refillable water bottle.
  • Any medicine your child usually carries.

A waste-free lunch

Our class is helping the farm stay tidy by taking home whatever we do not eat.

  • A reusable lunch bag or box and cloth napkin work great.
  • Fruit, hard-boiled eggs and snacks from the bulk store come in their own wrapping.
  • Pack just what your child can finish.

Extra water is a good idea on warm days. The farm has fresh water for refills.

Happy on the farm

  • The children stay with their teachers and supervisors, and with the farm guide, the whole time.
  • Farm tip: food and drinks stay out of the animal areas, so we will keep lunch and water bottles at our lunch spot.
  • Farm tip: we wash hands with soap and water after visiting the animals and before eating. Hand sanitizer alone is not enough.
  • Farm tip: we keep hands away from our mouths while with the animals, and we leave the farm equipment to the farmers.
  • Honeybees and other insects like the farm too. If your child has an allergy to insects, straw or animals, please send their prescribed medicine and tell your child's teacher ahead of time.
  • Please also tell the teacher about any dietary needs.
  • Uniform and shoes can go straight in the wash when you get home.

See where food comes from

The farm market will be open during our visit, full of seasonal Ontario fruits and vegetables. It is a lovely way to talk about local farms and the seasons.
